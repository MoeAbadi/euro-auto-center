About Us

Genuine European parts, delivered with precision.

Euro Auto Center is an automotive parts distributor based in Ras Al Khaimah, UAE. We specialize in European OEM and quality aftermarket parts and supply workshops, dealers, and distributors across the Middle East & Africa.

We pair a strong supply network with structured parts data, so every order is identified correctly, sourced quickly, and delivered with full traceability.

  • Genuine and quality-approved brands only
  • Accurate part identification by OE number and VIN
  • Regional reach from a strategic UAE base
