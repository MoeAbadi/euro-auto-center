About Euro Auto Center

A European auto parts distributor serving workshops, dealers and distributors across the Middle East & Africa.

Who We Are

Genuine European parts, delivered with precision.

Euro Auto Center is headquartered in Beirut, Lebanon, with a regional branch in Ras Al Khaimah, UAE. We specialize in European OEM and quality aftermarket parts and supply customers across the Middle East & Africa.

We combine a strong supply network of 30 authorized brands with structured parts data, so every order is identified correctly, sourced quickly, and delivered with full traceability.

  • Genuine and quality-approved brands only
  • Accurate part identification by OE number and VIN
  • Two offices covering the Levant and the Gulf
